# 增支部十法集 · 原文

## 一、文獻概述

《十法集》（Dasakanipāta，SuttaCentral 编号 AN10，https://suttacentral.net/an10）為《增支部》（Aṅguttara Nikāya）之第十集，收錄以「十」為法數的經典二百餘經，內容涵蓋十善業道與十不善業道、十波羅蜜、十聖居法、十遍處、十事等。本集著名的 AN 10.176《純陀鐵匠子經》（Cunda Kammāraputta Sutta，PTS A v 263）：佛陀在波婆城純陀鐵匠的芒果園中，破斥西方婆羅門以水、火、日光、聖草等作淨的儀式主義，開示聖者律中的「淨化」在於遠離身三（殺生、偷盜、邪淫）、口四（妄語、兩舌、惡口、綺語）、意三（貪欲、瞋恚、邪見）之十不善業道，奉行十善業道——此即南傳佛教「十善業」的經典出處之一。（據 DN 16，此純陀即後來供養佛陀最後一餐者。）

## 二、核心段落選錄

> **原文**（AN 10.176，Thanissaro 比丘英譯，巴利原典 A v 263）：
> "Cunda, the purification rites declared by the brahmans of the Western lands... are one thing; the purification in the discipline of the noble ones is something else entirely. ... These, Cunda, are the ten courses of unskillful action. When a person is endowed with these ten courses of unskillful action, then even if he gets up at the proper time from his bed and touches the earth, he is still impure. ... Why is that? Because these ten courses of unskillful action are impure and cause impurity."

> **白話對照**：
> 「純陀啊，那些西方婆羅門所宣說的淨化儀式是一回事；聖者律中的淨化則完全是另一回事。……純陀啊，這就是十不善業道。當一個人具足這十不善業道時，即使他按時起身、以手觸地，他仍是不淨的……為什麼？因為這十不善業道本身即是不淨、且導致不淨。」

## 三、重要名句

> **名句一**（AN 10.176 十善業道）：
> "These, Cunda, are the ten courses of skillful action. When a person is endowed with these ten courses of skillful action, then even if he gets up at the proper time from his bed and touches the earth, he is still pure. ... Why is that? Because these ten courses of skillful action are pure and cause purity. Furthermore, as a result of being endowed with these ten courses of skillful action, [rebirth among] the devas is declared, [rebirth among] human beings is declared — that or any other good destination."
> 「純陀啊，這就是十善業道。當一個人具足這十善業道時，即使他不觸地、不觸濕牛糞、不觸青草、不事火、不向日合掌、不於日暮時下水沐浴三次，他也仍然是清淨的。為什麼？因為這十善業道本身即是淨、且導致淨。再者，具足這十善業道的果報，經中說是生於天界或人間——彼處或其他任何善趣。」

> **名句二**（AN 10.176 正見）：
> "He has right view and is not warped in the way he sees things: 'There is what is given, what is offered, what is sacrificed. There are fruits & results of good & bad actions. There is this world & the next world. There is mother & father. There are spontaneously reborn beings; there are brahmans & contemplatives who, faring rightly & practicing rightly, proclaim this world & the next after having directly known & realized it for themselves.'"
> 「他具有正見，見解不歪曲：『有布施、有供養、有祭祀；有善惡業的果報；有此世、有他世；有母、有父；有化生眾生；有正行正修而於此世他世自證知並宣說的沙門婆羅門。』」
