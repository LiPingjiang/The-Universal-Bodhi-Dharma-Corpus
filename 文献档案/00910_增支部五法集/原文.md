# 增支部五法集 · 原文

## 一、文獻概述

《五法集》（Pañcakanipāta，SuttaCentral 编号 AN5，https://suttacentral.net/an5）為《增支部》（Aṅguttara Nikāya）之第五集，收錄以「五」為法數的經典約二百六十餘經，內容涵蓋五根（信進念定慧）、五力、五戒、五欲功德、五蓋、五下分結、五種失念之法等。本集最著名的經典之一為 AN 5.28《三摩地分經》（Samādhiṅga Sutta，PTS A iii 25），佛陀在此經中以浴粉、湧泉、蓮池、白布四喻，開示「具五支的聖正定」——即四禪的次第與第五支「觀察所緣善取於心」，並說明由此定力可證得六神通，包括漏盡智。

## 二、核心段落選錄

> **原文**（AN 5.28，Thanissaro 比丘英譯，巴利原典 A iii 25）：
> "There is the case where a monk — quite withdrawn from sensuality, withdrawn from unskillful qualities — enters and remains in the first jhāna: rapture and pleasure born from withdrawal, accompanied by directed thought and evaluation. He permeates and pervades, suffuses and fills this very body with the rapture and pleasure born from withdrawal. There is nothing of his entire body unpervaded by rapture and pleasure born from withdrawal.
> Just as if a skilled bathman or bathman's apprentice would pour bath powder into a brass basin and knead it together, sprinkling it again and again with water, so that his ball of bath powder — saturated, moisture-laden, permeated within and without — would nevertheless not drip; even so, the monk permeates, suffuses and fills this very body with the rapture and pleasure born of withdrawal."

> **白話對照**：
> 「在此，比丘從諸欲遠離、從不善法遠離之後，進入並安住於初禪——遠離所生之喜與樂，伴隨著尋與伺。他以遠離所生的喜與樂浸潤、充遍、周遍、充滿此身；無有全身任何一分不被遠離所生的喜與樂所浸潤。
> 猶如熟練的浴師或浴師的弟子，把浴粉倒入銅盆，加水揉搓、再三灑水，使浴粉團內外飽含水濕、浸透而滴水不漏；同樣地，比丘以遠離所生的喜與樂浸潤、充遍、周遍、充滿此身，無有全身任何一分不被遠離所生的喜與樂所浸潤。」

## 三、重要名句

> **名句一**（AN 5.28 第四禪喻）：
> "Just as if a man were sitting wrapped from head to foot with a white cloth so that there would be no part of his body to which the white cloth did not extend; even so, the monk sits, permeating his body with a pure, bright awareness. There is nothing of his entire body unpervaded by pure, bright awareness."
> 「猶如有人以白布從頭到腳裹身，無有一處身體不被白布覆蓋；同樣地，比丘而坐，以清淨皎潔之念遍潤此身，無有全身任何一分不被清淨皎潔之念所遍潤。」

> **名句二**（AN 5.28 結語）：
> "When a monk has developed and pursued the five-factored noble right concentration in this way, then whichever of the six higher knowledges he turns his mind to know and realize, he can witness them for himself whenever there is an opening."
> 「當比丘如此修習、多修具五支之聖正定時，則六上人法之中，無論他心向於何法而欲證知，皆能於現法自證、隨時通達。」
