# 增支部七法集 · 原文

## 一、文獻概述

《七法集》（Sattakanipāta，SuttaCentral 编号 AN7，https://suttacentral.net/an7）為《增支部》（Aṅguttara Nikāya）之第七集，收錄以「七」為法數的經典約一百三十餘經，內容涵蓋七覺支（念、擇法、精進、喜、輕安、定、捨）、七財、七種人、七種布施等。本集最著名的經典之一為 AN 7.63《城邑經》（Nagara Sutta，PTS A iv 106）：佛陀以具足七種城防資具與四種食物儲備的邊境王城為喻，說明聖弟子若具足七種正法（信、慚、愧、多聞、精進、念、慧），並隨意自在地獲得四種禪那，便不被魔、不被魔界所勝。此經後半的「王城」比喻，與法句經注等所載佛陀於菩提樹下所悟「城邑喻」相互呼應。

## 二、核心段落選錄

> **原文**（AN 7.63，Thanissaro 比丘英譯，巴利原典 A iv 106）：
> "Just as the royal frontier fortress has a foundation post — deeply rooted, well embedded, immovable, & unshakable — for the protection of those within and to ward off those without; in the same way a disciple of the noble ones has conviction, is convinced of the Tathagata's Awakening: 'Indeed, the Blessed One is worthy and rightly self-awakened, consummate in knowledge & conduct, well-gone, a knower of the cosmos, an unexcelled trainer of those persons ready to be tamed, teacher of human & divine beings, awakened, blessed.' With conviction as his foundation post, the disciple of the noble ones abandons what is unskillful, develops what is skillful, abandons what is blameworthy, develops what is blameless, and looks after himself with purity."

> **白話對照**：
> 「正如邊境王城有深埋堅固、不動不搖的城基柱，以保護城內之人、抵禦外敵；同樣地，聖弟子具足信，對如來的正覺深信不疑：『世尊確是阿羅漢、正等正覺者、明行足、善逝、世間解、無上士、調御丈夫、天人師、佛、世尊。』以信為城基柱，聖弟子捨斷不善、修習善法，捨斷有過失之法、修習無過失之法，以清淨自護。」

## 三、重要名句

> **名句一**（AN 7.63 慧為城牆）：
> "Just as the royal frontier fortress has ramparts — high & thick & completely covered with plaster — for the protection of those within and to ward off those without; in the same way a disciple of the noble ones is discerning, endowed with discernment of arising and passing away — noble, penetrating, leading to the right ending of stress. With discernment as his covering of plaster, the disciple of the noble ones abandons what is unskillful, develops what is skillful... and looks after himself with purity."
> 「正如王城有高厚而塗灰泥的城牆，護內而禦外；同樣地，聖弟子具慧，擁有生滅之智——聖智、穿透智、導向苦之正滅之智。以慧為塗牆之泥，聖弟子捨斷不善、修習善法……以清淨自護。」

> **名句二**（AN 7.63 結語）：
> "When a disciple of the noble ones is endowed with these seven true qualities and can obtain at will — without difficulty, without trouble — these four jhānas, heightened mental states that provide a pleasant abiding in the here-&-now, he is said to be a disciple of the noble ones who can't be undone by Māra, can't be undone by the Evil One."
> 「當聖弟子具足這七種正法，又能無難無求得心自在地獲得此四種禪那——現法樂住的高等心境界——時，這位聖弟子便稱為不被魔所勝、不被魔界所勝之人。」
